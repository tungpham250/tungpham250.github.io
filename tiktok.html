Connect your TikTok account to CrewReel

Kết nối tài khoản TikTok với CrewReel

CrewReel will request these permissions:

  • user.info.basic – read your display name and avatar to confirm the connected account.
  • video.upload – send finished videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published automatically; you review, edit and post each video yourself in the TikTok app.
Connect TikTok

You can disconnect at any time in TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
